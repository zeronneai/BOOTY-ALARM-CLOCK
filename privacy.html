Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Booty Alarm Clock is designed with your privacy in mind. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data with third parties. All processing happens locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

Booty Alarm Clock does not collect any personally identifiable information. The app operates entirely on your device without requiring an account or registration.

2. Camera Access

The app requests access to your device's camera solely to enable the workout rep-detection feature. Specifically:

  • Camera data is processed in real time on your device using your browser's built-in APIs.
  • No video, images, or camera data are ever recorded, stored, uploaded, or transmitted to any server.
  • Camera access is only active while you are using the workout feature and is released immediately after.
  • You can deny camera access at any time through your device settings. Core alarm functionality will remain available.

3. Microphone & Audio

The app plays alarm sounds and workout audio using your device's audio output. No microphone access is requested or used.

4. Notifications

The app may request permission to send local notifications to alert you when your alarm triggers. These notifications are generated entirely on your device and are not routed through any external server. No notification content is collected or logged.

5. Local Storage

The app stores your alarm settings and preferences locally on your device (via browser localStorage / PWA storage). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

6. No Third-Party Services

Booty Alarm Clock does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, or data collection services. We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook SDK, or any similar tools.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13. Since the app collects no personal data from any user, it is inherently compliant with COPPA and similar regulations.

8. Data Security

Because we do not collect or transmit any personal data, there is no risk of your data being compromised through our systems. All data remains on your device under your control.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no data to access, correct, or delete. If you have any questions or concerns, please contact us directly.
